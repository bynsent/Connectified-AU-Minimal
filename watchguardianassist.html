WATCH GUARDIAN ASSIST

Watch Guardian Assist is purpose-built for care environments where safety and dignity go hand in hand. Through smart alerts, secure connectivity, and intuitive interfaces, the platform supports both daily wellbeing and critical response. Whether in aged care facilities, hospitals, or private homes, Watch Guardian Assist ensures every user stays protected—and every carer stays informed.

Key Features

Alerts & Confirmation: Seamlessly sends reminders and lets users mark doses as taken; caregivers remain informed with status updates.

Prescription Renewal Notifications: Ensure continuity of care with timely reminders for upcoming refills and appointments.

Scheduled Welfare Checks: Automate check‑ins throughout the day and maintain activity logs for confidence and transparency.

Tiered Duress Alarms: From Green (non‑urgent) to Amber (escalation) to Red (emergency), offer graduated alerts with confirmations and vibrations.

Auto‑Answer Whitelist: Pre‑approved contacts can connect directly to the wearer’s watch, even if they can’t actively accept the call.

Live Location & Safe Zone Monitoring: Track movement and receive notifications if a wearer exits predefined areas—perfect for assisted living facilities.

History & Care Logs: Access comprehensive records of medication, alarms, and welfare check data to support decisions and share with healthcare providers.



Use Cases

Aged Care Facilities: Efficiently manage medication schedules, monitor movement within safe zones, and respond to emergencies with subtle signals.

Home-Based Assisted Living: Families stay close, even remotely—insights, reminders, and alerts all from the palm of their hand.

Hospitals & Rehabilitation Centres: Support swift response and documentation—without disrupting patient routines.
