Networking Solutions That Keep You Connected

Empower your business with state-of-the-art networking solutions powered by Connectified and our trusted partners—Peplink, Cradlepoint, and Milesight. Together, we deliver high-performance, secure and scalable connectivity tailored to your unique operational needs.

Partnering for Excellence

Peplink: Renowned for its robust and reliable SD-WAN technology, Peplink provides seamless multi-network connectivity, making it ideal for enterprise-grade networks and IoT applications.

Cradlepoint part of Ericsson: As pioneers in 5G and LTE solutions, Cradlepoint, powered by Ericsson, offers high-performance wireless routers and cloud-managed networking tools that cater to the demands of modern businesses requiring speed and scalability

Milesight: Specialising in innovative IoT and AI-driven devices, Milesight combines cutting-edge hardware with intelligent monitoring and data analysis, perfect for industries like smart cities, logistics and healthcare.

Teltonika: With a reputation for durable and versatile networking hardware, Teltonika provides reliable routers and IoT solutions tailored for industrial and enterprise applications.

Samsung & Samsung Knox: Leveraging Samsung's advanced technology and the industry-leading Samsung Knox platform, Connectified ensures enterprise-grade security, reliability and manageability across all connected devices.

By leveraging these industry leaders, Connectified ensures your network infrastructure remains at the forefront of technology.
