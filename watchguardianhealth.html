Empower Smarter Care with
WATCHGUARDIAN HEALTH

The WatchGuardian Health platform is a breakthrough monitoring solution developed by Connectified for Samsung Galaxy wearables. By harnessing advanced sensors and secure integration with Samsung Knox, it delivers live and historical health insights—independently, without the need for a paired phone. From ECG and heart rate to blood oxygen and body composition, WatchGuardian Health transforms wearables into reliable tools for clinical care, research, and everyday wellbeing.

Designed for diverse environments—from aged care facilities and hospitals to research labs and individual users—the platform empowers providers and patients alike with real-time health data, secure reporting, and scalable management. Whether it’s tracking vitals during clinical trials or supporting day-to-day health monitoring, WatchGuardian Health ensures actionable insights are always within reach.

WatchGuardian Health captures critical health metrics

including ECG, heart rate, blood oxygen, body composition, and skin temperature — in real time.

Data is presented instantly through a secure dashboard, allowing users, clinicians, and researchers

to make informed decisions faster. Whether used for routine monitoring or clinical observation,

WatchGuardian Health provides the visibility you need —

when it matters most.

Engineered for independence, WatchGuardian Health operates seamlessly without requiring a paired smartphone.

All data collected from Samsung wearables is transmitted and stored via Samsung Knox,

ensuring enterprise-grade encryption, device integrity, and compliance with modern healthcare standards.

Your health data stays private, protected, and always under your control — accessible only to authorised users and systems.

Simplify data management with automated collection, time-stamped reports,

and export-ready formats compatible with healthcare and research systems.

WatchGuardian Health organises both live and historical readings into structured visual reports,

helping you track progress, spot anomalies, and identify trends over time.

Ideal for clinical research teams, aged care providers, and individual users

— because data should work for you, not overwhelm you.

From hospitals and aged care facilities to corporate wellness programs and medical research,

WatchGuardian Health adapts to a wide range of use cases.

The scalable platform supports single users or entire organisations,

offering configurable access levels and multi-device management.

Whether you're monitoring one patient or a thousand, WatchGuardian Health grows with you

— providing real-time insights and reliability at every level.
